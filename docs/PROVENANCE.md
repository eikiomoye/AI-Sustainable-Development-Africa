# Provenance

## Source

The companion was prepared from the published PDF supplied by the user, `Article_Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Development.pdf`. The file has a cover sheet followed by 32 printed article pages. Source identity and publication details are recorded in `source_metadata.json`. The source PDF was not edited and is not redistributed in this package.

The title page was inspected visually to confirm the title, author order, publication date and equal-contribution statement. The author contributions and framework limitations were checked in the text. Printed article page numbers are used in the catalogues, and bibliography entries additionally record the physical PDF page number.

## Bibliography

The final reference list occupies printed pages 29-32, corresponding to physical PDF pages 30-33. The 72 numbered entries were extracted in reading order. Running headers, DOI footers and the publisher disclaimer were excluded. Whitespace was normalised, typographic dashes were converted to ASCII hyphens and Unicode compatibility normalisation expanded ligatures such as fi/fl.

Line-wrap hyphens, names and other extraction artefacts may remain. Every record is marked `extracted_from_article_not_independently_verified`. In particular, the displayed [CrossRef] and [PubMed] markers are retained as source text; their hyperlink destinations were not reconstructed or guessed.

All reference numbers from 1 to 72 appear exactly once. This establishes structural completeness, not independent verification of each citation. The bibliography is not used as a substitute for the 26-study synthesis dataset.

## Summaries and worksheet

The framework, dimensions, domain summaries and future priorities are new companion paraphrases anchored to Sections 4, 8 and 9 and Table 4. The worksheet is a newly created blank discussion aid with fields for evidence, observations, stakeholder input, ownership and review dates. Its state vocabulary and utility are companion additions and were not validated in the article.

The PRISMA counts and full-text exclusion totals are transcribed reported values from Section 3.3. No individual record, exclusion decision, study identifier or screening history is invented. The raw screening/extraction records and supplementary PRISMA checklist were not supplied.

## Checks

Validation checks reference-number continuity, CSV/JSON equality, source identity, citation author order, expected catalogue dimensions and study-selection arithmetic. The command-line utility is checked on searches, a blank worksheet, a hypothetical worksheet entry and invalid input. The hypothetical entry is used only in a temporary validation file and is not included as research evidence.

Results are recorded in `VALIDATION.json`. There is no field deployment, model training, country assessment or empirical framework validation in this companion. `SOURCE_NOTES.md` documents unresolved source issues, including Table 2 citation mismatches and the distinction between the stated publication focus and later sources in the bibliography.
